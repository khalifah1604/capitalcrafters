How it works

Three structures. One clear principle.

Our return comes from a documented trade, partnership or asset lease, not compounding interest.

Murabaha

Trade-based financing for inventory and business assets, with the cost and agreed markup disclosed up front.

Musharaka

A partnership structure where capital and business performance are linked through a pre-agreed profit share.

Ijarah

Asset financing through a lease arrangement, including lease-to-own options for eligible equipment and vehicles.

How We Started

A vision born from watching good businesses fail for the wrong reasons.

Capital Crafters Limited was founded when our leadership team observed a critical gap in Nigeria's financial landscape: hardworking entrepreneurs with viable, profitable businesses were being crushed by high-interest facilities that consumed their margins before they could reinvest.

We believed there was a better way. Drawing on the principles of ethical finance and non-interest investment, we built a model that aligns our success directly with yours — when your business grows, that is how we benefit.

Today we have deployed over ₦2.4 billion in non-interest capital to more than 500 Nigerian SMEs, from single-shop retailers in Kano to mid-scale manufacturers in Lagos, transforming businesses and livelihoods across the country.
